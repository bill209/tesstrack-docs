Privacy Policy

TessTrack

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Overview

TessTrack is a personal vehicle cost-tracking app for Tesla owners. This policy explains what data the app accesses, where it is stored, and who can see it.

What data TessTrack accesses

To display cost-of-ownership information, TessTrack retrieves the following from the Tessie API for the vehicle(s) you connect:

  • Charging sessions and charging cost data
  • Drive and consumption history (efficiency, mileage)
  • Battery health measurements
  • Vehicle state and odometer readings
  • Tire pressure and firmware alert status

This data belongs to you and is retrieved directly from Tessie, a third-party service you separately authorize with your own account and vehicle credentials.

Where your data is stored

Your Tessie API key and vehicle identification numbers (VINs) are stored in your personal iCloud account, not inside the app bundle and not on any server operated by us. Vehicle data retrieved from Tessie is used to render the app's screens and is not persisted on our infrastructure — we do not operate a backend server for TessTrack.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not have access to your Tessie account, API key, or vehicle data ourselves — it stays between your device, your iCloud account, and Tessie.

Third-party services

TessTrack retrieves vehicle data via the Tessie API, using credentials you provide and control. Your use of Tessie is subject to Tessie's own privacy policy and terms.

Children's privacy

TessTrack is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "last updated" date.
