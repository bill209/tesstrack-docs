Frequently asked questions

What does TessTrack do?

TessTrack pulls data for your Tesla vehicles — charging sessions, efficiency, battery health, odometer readings, and maintenance alerts — to help you track the total cost of ownership over time.

Where does my data come from?

TessTrack retrieves vehicle data via the Tessie API. Your API credentials are stored securely on your device and are never shared with third parties.

Which vehicles are supported?

TessTrack supports switching between multiple Tesla vehicles connected to your Tessie account.

How do I report an issue?

Email support@ozzis.us with a description of the issue, and screenshots if possible. We aim to respond within a few business days.
